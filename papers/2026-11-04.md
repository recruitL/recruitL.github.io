<p>We present a calculation of the first-order metric perturbation arising from a point mass moving on a circular, equatorial orbit in Kerr spacetime. <p>We investigate the quasinormal modes (QNMs) and time-domain response of a massless, minimally coupled test scalar field on static, spherically symmetric covariant loop quantum black hole (cLQBH) backgrounds. <p>The Rényi entropies of a ball are computed by a conformal defect, the Rényi defect, dual in holography to a hyperbolic black hole. At an index $n_*$ its charged operator merges with its alternate-quantization partner, and the defect loses conformality. Beyond $n_*$ it flows with no source to a conformal defect that breaks the U(1) symmetry; below $n_*$ that defect needs a symmetry-breaking source, so at large $N$, conformality loss makes the breaking spontaneous.
