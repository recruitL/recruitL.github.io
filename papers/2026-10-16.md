<summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>We formulate and numerically solve the nonlinear Teukolsky equations through third order, obtaining a first-principles calculation of cubic quasinormal-mode amplitudes for Kerr black holes. Our approach combines lower-order metric reconstruction in split light-cone gauges with a hyperboloidal, compactified formulation solved on complex radial contours. Spin-dependent complex coupling coefficients fix the cubic amplitudes from products of their linear parent amplitudes. We apply these predictions to a nonprecessing numerical-relativity waveform, including cubic products of the $(2,2)$ fundamental mode, its first overtone, and their conjugates. The predicted cubic terms improve agreement over selected early-ringdown fitting windows and shift inferred linear-overtone amplitudes without adding fitting parameters. $3M$ after the peak of $|h_{22}|$, the summed cubic contribution reaches approximately $38\%$ of the total $h_{22}$ signal; $M$ is the initial total mass. These results identify an appreciable cubic response in early ringdown.</p>

<p>Two black-hole merger events may originate from sequential mergers in a pre-existing hierarchical black-hole triple if their spatial localizations overlap and the remnant mass of the first merger is compatible with a component mass of the second. <summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>Supermassive black hole binaries (SMBHB) are expected at the center of galaxies as a result of galaxy mergers. The dynamics and evolution of SMBHBs shed light on the evolution and formation of galaxies, the gravitational wave background and the nature of gravity. <p>Recent LHAASO observations indicate that microquasars and massive star clusters contribute to Galactic cosmic rays, and likely also to the pre-knee spectral hardening. Their markedly different astrophysical environments, compared with supernova remnants (SNRs), may imprint distinct cosmic-ray abundances. We investigate the spectra and composition of new-source cosmic rays under two abundance scenarios: Case-1, the SNR-like composition with enhanced heavy nuclei, and Case-2, the surface abundances of microquasar companion stars. We constrain injection spectral indices using LHAASO proton, helium, and all-particle spectra, and use their data for model discrimination.
