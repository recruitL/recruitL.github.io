<p>The parameterized post-Newtonian (PPN) approach is the state of the art formalism for performing theory independent tests of weak-field gravity, and for constraining possible deviations from Einstein&#x27;s theory. <summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>Future interferometers may detect a gravitational-wave (GW) signal from a cosmological first-order phase transition.
