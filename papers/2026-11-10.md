<p><strong>相关性：</strong>匹配关注项：gr-qc 相关方向。 这是 gr-qc 每日新增或更新条目。</p>

<p><strong>动作：</strong>建议扫读摘要和结论，必要时加入阅读列表。</p>

<summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>The Einstein equations constrain the transverse Taylor series at a degenerate Killing horizon, but even complete knowledge of that series need not determine a smooth spacetime: a nonzero difference can vanish to every order at the horizon. We show that this ambiguity disappears when the limiting metric on the space of Killing orbits admits a spacelike graph over a compact horizon section. In dimensions $d\geq4$, smooth vacuum Einstein germs with the same marked transverse jets are then locally isometric on a two-sided neighborhood of the whole section. The proof combines a terminal wave-map gauge with energy estimates on the orbit cylinder and yields a finite weighted matching criterion. For unwarped horizons with positive-Einstein sections, a condition on the full Lichnerowicz spectrum reduces the smooth classification to parallel first transverse data. All such data have convergent multiwarped Einstein realizations, including examples whose sections have negative sectional-curvature directions. We also obtain a finite-regularity version for round sections. The continuation argument extends to four-dimensional Einstein--Maxwell theory and gives smooth charged-Nariai rigidity. Finally, rotating Nariai data admit the required spacelike graph throughout the regular branch; an all-order tensor estimate yields smooth Kerr--de Sitter or rotating-Nariai rigidity for $a^2/r_0^2\leq1/8$, without assuming ambient axial symmetry.</p>
</details>

<summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>Many studies of animal vocalizations require clean recordings, but animals are usually recorded in the field, with background noise from weather, vehicles, and other animals. Animal vocalization denoising is challenged by the scarcity of clean training data and the extreme diversity of animal vocalizations. Classical spectral gates leave residual noise, especially without voice activity detection (VAD), while detector-free neural denoisers designed for bioacoustics remove less noise. We show that a general-purpose source separation foundation model, SAM-Audio, removes more noise than either of these types of methods, when used without training and prompted with time spans from a simple animal VAD. We find that SI-SDR has limited utility in our setting as it penalizes regenerated output and conflates noise removal with call fidelity, a trade-off our separate metrics reveal. CallCleaner leaves 11 dB less noise around the call than Biodenoising, the leading neural bioacoustic denoiser with public weights, and among fast, CPU-capable systems it pushes the Pareto frontier of noise removal vs. call fidelity, without requiring a detector. We release code and weights for CallCleaner, which enables fast, private, local denoising of single-caller recordings without specialized hardware.</p>

<summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>Existing evaluations often test persona expression rather than whether persona information persists in extended decision-making. We introduce THEATRON, a controlled benchmark that uses financial replay to evaluate persona persistence in long-horizon sequential decision-making. In these environments, models conditioned on fixed behavioral-finance personas observe shared daily market evidence and trajectory-specific account-state feedback and make sequential decisions over multi-month trajectories. <summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>Atmospheric neutrinos span a broad range of energies and baselines, offering a complementary probe of leptonic CP violation.
