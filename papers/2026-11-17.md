<p>Newtonian gravity, in the light of the Equivalence Principle, is best understood as a localization of Galilean inertial structure --- or at least, of the part of Galilean inertial structure that defines rectilinear acceleration. <summary>英文摘要</summary>
<p>We investigate spherically symmetric spacetimes sourced by matter with energy density $ρ\propto 1/r^{4}$ and equation of state $p_r = ωρ$. We derive the most general solution of Einstein&#x27;s equations for this class of sources, obtaining black holes, naked singularities and traversable wormholes, parametrized by the integration constants and $ω$.
